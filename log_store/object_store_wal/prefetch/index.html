Segment fetching shared by local reads and remote recovery.

Constants§

READ_PREFETCH_BYTES 🔒
Bytes of decoded segments that all reads of one store, or of one remote source prefix, together fetch ahead. A segment counts as its length, which bounds its payloads, plus a Record per entry. A read always fetches the segment it yields next, as a read that fetched one segment at a time did, and a later segment only while it fits in what is left, so regions replayed in parallel hold one segment each, up to about max_batch_bytes or more for one oversized append, plus at most these bytes, however many regions open at once. While a fetch copies the payloads out of the downloaded segment it briefly holds the segment’s length once more.
READ_PREFETCH_DEPTH 🔒
Number of segments a read fetches ahead of the one whose entries it yields. On S3 a replay that fetched each segment after the previous one spent about 30 ms per object; with eight fetches in flight a segment arrives about every 4 ms. The segments fetched ahead also take their bytes from READ_PREFETCH_BYTES.

Functions§

fetch_segment 🔒
Fetches and decodes the segment entry describes in the object object_seq. A segment that does not decode is fetched once more, since the download rather than the object may be damaged; when it still does not decode the error is returned as crate::error::Error::InvalidWalObject. A failed fetch is returned as is.
segments 🔒
Fetches segments in order, retaining prefetch permits until the consumer advances. Errors are yielded for the caller to apply its own recovery policy.
spawn_fetch_segment 🔒
Runs fetch_segment on a task of its own, so the fetch proceeds while the read is busy with earlier segments, and returns the outcome with permit. The task holds the permit, so the reserved bytes are released only with the buffers of the fetch. Dropping the returned future, as a dropped read does, aborts the task; a task that panics fails the fetch with crate::error::Error::WalSegmentFetch.
try_reserve_prefetch_bytes 🔒
Takes from budget the bytes the segment entry describes holds once decoded, if they are free: its length plus a Record per entry.
