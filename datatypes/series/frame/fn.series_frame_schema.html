Builds the one frame schema family: raw or evaluated, keyed or explicit.

Everything the wire states is derived from the two things a producer actually holds. The evaluation grid comes from schema.eval_grid, where a schema without one describes raw samples and a schema with one describes values addressed on that grid, so content and time mode can never contradict the columns they describe. labels decides identity, and with it whether the frame carries the name/labels columns: a key frame keeps its labels encoded in read_key and must declare the LabelDecodeSpec that decodes them, while an explicit frame carries complete labels and must not declare one.

This is the only place the reserved greptime.series.* text is written, so a producer and a reader cannot spell one frame two ways.
