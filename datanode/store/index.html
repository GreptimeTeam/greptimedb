Constants§

PROBE_CLEANUP_TIMEOUT 🔒
The budget of the best-effort cleanup after a timed-out probe, kept short so an unresponsive store cannot block startup a second time.
PROBE_CONTENT 🔒
The content written by probe_object_store.
PROBE_DIR 🔒
The directory that holds the objects created by probe_object_store.
PROBE_STAGGER 🔒
The maximum random delay before a probe starts. It is short enough that startup waits at most this delay, and it spreads out the probes of providers that share an object storage.
PROBE_SUFFIX 🔒
The content appended by probe_object_store to exercise read-modify-write.
PROBE_TIMEOUT 🔒
The budget of one probe_object_store call, including the retries an object store makes on top of its 30s request timeout.
