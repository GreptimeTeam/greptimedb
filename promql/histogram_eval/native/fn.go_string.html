pub(super) fn go_string(h: &HistogramView<'_>) -> Result<String>
Expand description

Go FloatHistogram.String, the form count_values writes a histogram value in: the count and sum, then the buckets holding observations from the lowest up – negative, zero, positive – each as [lower,upper):count with its bounds’ inclusiveness, every number in Go’s %g. Unlike all_buckets, the buckets keep the bounds their index gives, unclipped by the zero bucket.
