The figures one field’s phases are charged from.

The presence of each side and of the points both are present at are three flags per grid point; the one side’s groups are a key, the values on(...) matching copies and a member list per series; one series of the many side is looked up by a key of its own; and a field reports at most three messages, each a fixed frame around one rendering. The duplicate scan’s list is not part of these tables: a group of one member collects nothing, and a wider group’s list is charged by overlap as it grows. The pairing’s own terms are read from the widest series of each side rather than from every pair, since one pairing’s label set replaces the one before it.
