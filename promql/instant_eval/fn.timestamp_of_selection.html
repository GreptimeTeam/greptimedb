timestamp(v) over an instant selector: at each point the selection holds, the original time of the selected sample in seconds, not the time it was looked up at, so an offset or @ moves which sample is selected but not the time reported. The metric name, __type__ and __unit__ are dropped.

indices and evaluation_timestamps are a selection of time and fields as select_instants returns one: grid positions increasing, rows not decreasing. schema holds the evaluation grid the timestamps lie on and one field per field of fields. A field without a sample in the selected row has no result there.

timestamp(x @ t) selects at t for every point, so its selection repeats one row. This kernel reports the sample the caller selected and does not decide the selector’s time modifiers: the native Instant caller selects the sample under the intersection of the ordinary anchored selector window and the timestamp function’s unshifted window. info() retains ordinary selector offset behavior.
