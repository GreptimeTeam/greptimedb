Expand description

One series over an instant selection, each field holding value(sample, original time) where the selected row has a sample for it.

The selection is indices and evaluation_timestamps as select_instants returns them: one source row and one grid timestamp per selected point, with positions increasing and rows not decreasing. An @ selection, which reads one row at every point, repeats that row; it is the caller’s to build. A selection off the grid, going back, or naming a row the series does not have fails.
