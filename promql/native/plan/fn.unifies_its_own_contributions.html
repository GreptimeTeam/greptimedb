pub fn unifies_its_own_contributions(node: &NativeNode) -> Option<bool>
Expand description

Whether node hands over one logical series per canonical label set, or several.

This is the rule the runtime collectors use to decide whether a consumer above node has to collect what node emits. RuntimeNode::build in execute and the Window and limitk collectors share this predicate so their decisions cannot disagree.

Three answers, because what a consumer may do about a contribution carries two facts and a boundary answers neither:

  • Some(true): the output is one series per canonical label set, established here, so a consumer that needs one unified series already has it and collects nothing.
  • Some(false): the output may hold several series of one label set, and this node says so, so a consumer that needs one unified series collects them through MergeInput. A NativeExpr::Merge above such a node would make it stream an already-merged series and turn that collection off.
  • None: the output may hold several, and this node says nothing: it has neither a proof to offer nor a report to make. Only a boundary (NativeExpr::Input) answers this, and a node that hands its input’s series back unchanged passes the answer on. Runtime collectors leave this case alone because they collect only when the answer is Some(false).

The collection condition is matches!(answer, Some(false)): the Window MergeInput collector at runtime and the limitk collector both ask this predicate of their input.
