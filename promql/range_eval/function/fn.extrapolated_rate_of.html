The arithmetic of extrapolated_rate, over a window’s endpoints and the previous values of its resets.

Both callers reach it: the per-window form with a slice of the field, the batch form with the field’s own arrays. Keeping it in one place is what holds their results equal; the two differ only in how they find the endpoints.
