Expand description

Checks a series for the aggregate op folds, and returns its evaluated axis.

Every operation this route folds takes a histogram series: it counts the sample, skips it, or folds the payload. What a series must satisfy is therefore its axis, not its sample type – the fold itself is where a payload an operation cannot use is refused or dropped.
