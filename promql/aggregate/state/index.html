promql::aggregate

Module state 

Source
Expand description

Partial aggregation: the state one execution partition folds its series into, and the merge of such states into the final result.

A merge reassociates a serial fold, so results can differ from aggregate_float by rounding, and an overflow of finite values can move: a serial sum of [1e308, 1e308, -1e308] is +Inf, while the merge of [1e308] and [1e308, -1e308] is finite. stddev/stdvar differ more for nearly equal samples, since Prometheus’ uncompensated Welford update rounds the mean on every sample: for [1e16] and [1e16 + 2] Prometheus gives a variance of 2 and the merge the exact 1. Where the serial fold keeps the first of two equal values (min/max on 0 and -0), a merge keeps the one merged first.
