Constants§

MAX_GLOBAL_TOPK_FETCH 🔒
Largest fetch that gets one heap. The heap runs on one thread: with a filter passing 90% of 24M rows, it took about 1.6x as long as per-partition heaps at fetch 100000. Below that, fetch does not decide the outcome: with files split by time one heap wins or ties at fetch 1000 to 10000, and with files overlapping in time it loses up to 12% at each of them when the filter passes most rows.
