fn matching_payload_shape(

Expand description

Matches the narrow supported AVG(payload * payload) or SUM(payload * payload) shape and returns the join plus everything the rewrite needs from it.

Required shape: an inner ON equi-join whose group and payload columns map directly to the respective join sides, grouped by the left host and the right area, aggregated by a single AVG — or, for PayloadFunction::Sum, SUM — of the product of those two columns, with no DISTINCT, FILTER, ORDER BY, null treatment or extra argument. The product operands may be given in either order and may reach the aggregate through a projection that repeats them.
