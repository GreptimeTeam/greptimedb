Module factorized_join_agg 

Source
Expand description

Shape-based AVG(value * weight) and SUM(value * weight) join-aggregation rewrites.

Every rewrite turns ... JOIN ... GROUP BY host, area with a single aggregate into a join of two partial aggregates computed on one side of the join each, with a projection above the join reconstructing the aggregate. Admission is decided by the plan shape alone: the rule never inspects the data its inputs will read.

AVG(value * weight) / SUM(value * weight): the aggregate over the pairwise products is replaced by SUM(value) * SUM(weight) divided by COUNT(value) * COUNT(weight) (AVG) or used as it is (SUM). The counts are checked individually for zero; for AVG they are cast to Float64 before multiplication to avoid overflowing their Int64 representation.

§Approximation contract

The payload rewrite is opt-in rule state and shape-based: it does not inspect the payloads, so it cannot know how well they factorize.

  • Floating-point reassociation means the factorized result can differ from the pairwise baseline; on inputs with heavy cancellation, intermediate overflow, or subnormal/extreme magnitudes, it can differ arbitrarily, in magnitude and in sign.

That difference is accepted in this opt-in experiment mode: the opt-in switch and this documentation are the guard, not an admission classifier. Queries whose payloads may be ill-conditioned should keep the switch off.

One difference is removed for free: a zero numerator is normalized to +0.0 (a CASE in the reconstruction), so a cancellation the baseline evaluates to +0.0 cannot surface here as -0.0.

The rule is intentionally narrow: it matches only the join and group shapes described by matching_payload_shape. It is registered in the optimizer but remains inert unless enabled by config/session extension state.
